## 2. The 6-Element Funk Suite

### 1. Tiered Animated Streak Flames (Instead of Static Emojis)
Tracks consecutive on-time deliverable days with an animated vector flame that evolves over time:
* **Tier 1 (1–3 Days On-Time) — The Spark ⚡**: Pulsing electric-blue spark with subtle radiating wave rings.
* **Tier 2 (4–7 Days On-Time) — The Blaze 🔥**: Roaring orange/amber flame with rising ember particles.
* **Tier 3 (8+ Days On-Time) — The Supernova 🔮**: Intense violet/gold flame with an iridescent aura around the user avatar.
* *Streak Freeze Protection*: If an employee toggles **"On Leave"** in their profile, their streak is safely frozen until they return.

### 2. Spring-Draw SVG Checkmark
* Checking off a task does not just toggle a box.
* The circular boundary pulses outward, and the checkmark **draws itself along an SVG stroke path** with a slight tactile spring recoil, followed by a micro-confetti cannon burst.

### 3. Manager "Kudos" Reaction Stickers on Approval
When approving a deliverable, managers can 1-click attach an animated sticker:
* 🚀 **"Speed Demon"**: Shipped significantly ahead of deadline.
* 💎 **"Pixel Perfect"**: Zero revisions, flawless quality.
* 🧠 **"Big Brain"**: Clever problem solving or unblocking teammates.
* 👑 **"Chef's Kiss"**: Exceptional overall execution.
* *The Payoff*: Employee receives a celebratory animated modal with their earned badge.

### 4. "Inbox Zero / Daily Victory" Screen
When all tasks assigned for today are completed:
* The view smoothly transitions into a relaxing animated sunset lounge / hammock scene:
  > **“🎉 All Clear for Today!”**  
  > *4 deliverables crushed • Streak: 6 Days • You're all done!*
* Creates psychological closure to the workday and prevents burnout.

### 5. Tactile Sound Design (ASMR Mechanical & Bubble Feedback)
Subtle audio cues with a persistent **1-click Global Mute toggle** in the header/settings:
* **Task Created (`Enter`)**: Crisp mechanical keyboard switch click.
* **Task Checked**: Satisfying lo-fi bubble pop or warm wooden chime.
* **Blocker Resolved**: Victorious arcade coin / level-up chime.
* **Gentle Nudge**: Gentle, non-intrusive radar sonar ping.

### 6. Personal Workspace Skins (Themes)
Users can customize their workspace palette in Settings:
* 🕶️ **Obsidian Minimalist** (Default): High-contrast Apple-style dark glassmorphism.
* 🌌 **Cyberpunk Neon**: Matte black with electric cyan and hot-pink borders.
* 🍵 **Tokyo Matcha**: Soothing deep forest green, bamboo, and warm cream.
* 🌅 **Sunset Lo-Fi**: Dusk purple, warm peach, and amber evening glow.

### 7. Adaptive Pixel Mascot (Header Micro-Expressions)
A subtle animated character sitting in the app header reacting to the user's live status:
* **Morning / All Clear**: Calmly sipping hot coffee with rising steam ☕.
* **Task In Progress**: Wears headphones in deep focus mode 🎧.
* **Task Blocked**: Puts up a yellow construction warning cone 🚧.
* **All Tasks Done**: Relaxes in a hammock under a palm tree 🌴.
